# Content policy

**Draft.** This page is a draft that the store team and a lawyer are still reviewing. It is not in force until it is marked final, and it can change.

This policy covers an app or face itself, its assets, and its listing (name, summary, description, icon, screenshots and
captions). Reviewers apply it; the automated checks apply the parts a program can.

## Not allowed

- **Content that is illegal** where the store operates, or that helps with something illegal.
- **Hate**: content that attacks people for who they are (race, ethnicity, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, age, nationality), or praises such attacks.
- **Adult content**: sexual content, nudity and graphic violence. This is a watch for everyone.
- **Gambling with real money**, and anything that sells chance for money. Games that use only points are fine.
- **Harassment and impersonation**: pretending to be another person, an organisation, another app or the store itself;
  copying another developer's app or listing.
- **Deception**: a listing that says the app does something it does not, or an app that does something it does not say.
  Nothing may be hidden behind a timer, a date or a switch on a server.
- **Malware and abuse**: code that tries to leave the sandbox, wear out the watch or its battery on purpose, flood a server, or
  collect data the user was not told about.
- **Other people's work without a right to it** (see below).

## Games and emulators

An app may **not** contain, or download, game images or cartridge and disc dumps (`.gb`, `.gbc`, `.nes`, `.iso` and the like), or
console firmware (BIOS files). Emulator and player apps are possible only when the user brings their own files; the store's
own Game Boy support works this way.

## Names

An app, face or listing may not use a console maker's or a game's name, or imply that it is theirs: **no** "Game Boy",
"Nintendo", "Mario" and similar names, in the app's name, description or author. The [trademark rules](trademarks.md) say more.

## Other people's work

Use only what you made or have the right to use: pictures, sounds, fonts, code and text. Say in the source where the licence
comes from. Open-source code is welcome under its licence. If someone shows that you have no right to something, the store
removes it ([takedown](updates-and-takedown.md)).

## Content rating

Every listing declares a `content_rating`:

| Rating | Means |
|---|---|
| `everyone` | Nothing a young child should not see. At most cartoon or fantasy mischief |
| `teen` | Stylised violence or strong language that is not graphic. Nothing under "Not allowed" |

A reviewer may change the rating. An app that is only right for adults is not allowed in the store.

## Listing text

Short, true and in plain words. No keyword stuffing, no other apps' names, no prices (the store has no paid apps), no claims about
health or medical results the app cannot back up.
