# Developer agreement

**Draft.** This page is a draft that the store team and a lawyer are still reviewing. It is not in force until it is marked final, and it can change. **It is a template: the entity, the governing law and the contact address are placeholders to be filled in before it
is used, and it is not legal advice.**

This agreement is between **[the store operator: legal name and address]** (the "Store") and you, the person or organisation
that submits an app or watch face (the "Developer"). By submitting, you accept it. The version you accepted is recorded with
the date.

## 1. What you give the Store

You keep ownership of what you make. You give the Store a worldwide, free licence to copy, build, check, sign, host and distribute
your submission and its listing in the S3Wear store and the Companion app, to show screenshots and text from it, and to keep
copies for the history of the store. The licence lasts as long as the submission is in the store and for the time needed to remove
it everywhere.

## 2. Source and open source

The store builds from source, so the source must be public in the store repository, under a licence that lets the Store do what
section 1 says and lets other people read it. You choose the licence. Nothing in the submission may stop the Store from building it.
The Store never accepts a binary on its own.

## 3. Your promises

You promise that: you have the right to everything in the submission (code, pictures, sounds, fonts, text, names); it follows the
[content policy](content-policy.md), the [privacy rules](privacy-rules.md) and the [trademark rules](trademarks.md); the listing is
true; you will answer reports about the app; and the information in your profile is correct.

## 4. Data and privacy

You are responsible for the data your app collects, sends or keeps, and for following the law that applies to it. The Store does
not see that data. If you run a server for the app, you run it.

## 5. Free of charge

The store has no paid apps. Your submission is free to users. A link to a place to donate in the listing is allowed. You may
not charge for the app inside it.

## 6. Review, removal and changes

The Store may review, refuse, change the listing of, or remove a submission as the [updates, takedown and appeals](updates-and-takedown.md)
page says. It may also stop accepting submissions from a developer who repeatedly breaks the rules. You may ask the Store to remove
your submission at any time.

## 7. Developer licence

After your first approved submission, or on request, the Store may give you a **developer licence** for the Pro firmware for up to
two of your watches, so you can test. It is for testing, it ends if this agreement ends, and it is not transferable.

## 8. No warranty, limited liability

The store, the SDK, the simulator and the tools are provided as they are, without warranty, to the extent the law allows. The Store is
not liable for loss of data or income, or for damage to a device, that comes from using them, to the extent the law allows. The same
goes for what you provide to users: they get it from you under your licence.

## 9. Ending

You or the Store may end this agreement at any time by telling the other. Removal of a submission follows
section 6. Sections 1 (for the time stated), 3, 4, 8 and 10 continue after the agreement ends.

## 10. Law

This agreement is governed by the law of **[governing law and place]**, and its courts decide disputes, except where the law
gives you a right to go elsewhere.

## 11. Changes

The Store may change this agreement with at least 30 days' notice by message and on this page. If you do not agree, you can ask to
have your submissions removed before the change takes effect. Submitting after that means you accept the new version.
