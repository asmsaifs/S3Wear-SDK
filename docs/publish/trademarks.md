# Trademarks

**Draft.** This page is a draft that the store team and a lawyer are still reviewing. It is not in force until it is marked final, and it can change.

## The S3Wear name

"S3Wear" and its logo identify the watch, the firmware, the Companion app and the store. Developers may say that an app or face
works **with** S3Wear, and may not say it is S3Wear's own or approved by S3Wear.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|
| "Snake for S3Wear" | "S3Wear Snake" (reads as the store's own app) |
| "A calculator for the S3Wear watch" | "Official S3Wear calculator" |
| "Made with the S3Wear SDK" | Using the S3Wear logo as your app's icon |

Names that start with "S3Wear" or "S3W" are kept for the store's own apps, as are the ids `dev.s3wear.*`, `s3w.*`
and `com.s3wear.*`.

## Other people's names

Do not use someone else's trademark, product name, logo or character in an app's name, icon, description or screenshots in a way that
suggests they made it or approve it. This covers game and console names ("Game Boy", "Nintendo", "Mario"), phone and
watch makers, brands, and the names of other apps. A plain, fair mention of a product the app works with ("reads your Home
Assistant") is fine.

## Look-alikes

An app must not look so like another app, a system screen or the store that people could mistake it: no copying of another
developer's icon or screens, no fake system dialogs, no screens that look like the watch's own settings or lock.

## If a name is challenged

If a trademark owner asks the store to remove a name, the store asks the developer to change it. An app that does not change is
removed ([takedown](updates-and-takedown.md)).
